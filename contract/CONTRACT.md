## Scope of Work

**Core pages**
- **Home** — sticky top bar (Phone, Hours, Call Now, WhatsApp), hero, tagline, mission, enquiry CTA
- **About Us / About the Lab** — founders, partnership, vision, quality philosophy
- **Services** — Histopathology & Molecular Diagnostics (histopathology, cytology, IHC, molecular panels)
- **Quality & Accreditation** — NABL/CAP goals, SOPs, turnaround-time commitment

**Tests & reporting**
- **Test List** — tests presented with categories, symptoms/indications, and details; guided by the chatbot "find a test" flow (no pricing shown — pricing is handled via enquiry/contact)
- **Doctor & Patient Login** — redirects to your existing Reporting Portal (link only)

**Content & media**
- **Research & Publications**
- **Case Studies & Newsletter** — de-identified case studies published on the website and emailed to referring doctors
- **Gallery** — facility photos plus equipment/machines, their uses, and related information
- **Videos** — embedded YouTube section

**Patients & engagement**
- **Patients** — information plus a downloadable Patient Form (PDF); no online data storage
- **FAQ**, **Testimonials**, **Careers**

**Contact & extras**
- **Contact Us** — Google Maps, enquiry form, address, details
- **Social Media Integration**
- **Custom Rule-Based Chatbot** — answers common questions, guides users to WhatsApp / phone
