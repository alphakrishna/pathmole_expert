Building skills for the future of diagnostics

Bridging knowledge and practice

The PathMole Training Institute, an upcoming initiative of PATHMOLE EXPERT LLP, is being developed to provide practical, structured, and industry-relevant training for healthcare and laboratory professionals.

Modern diagnostics is changing rapidly. New technologies, evolving quality standards, molecular techniques, automation, and increasing clinical expectations require professionals to continuously update their knowledge and skills. Our aim is to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical laboratory experience through focused, hands-on learning.

Who we aim to train

Designed for every stage of a career

Our programmes will be designed for a broad range of healthcare and laboratory professionals.

  • Pathology residents and postgraduate students
  • Laboratory technicians and technologists
  • Pathologists and other healthcare professionals
  • Medical and life-science graduates
  • Laboratory managers and quality professionals
  • Professionals interested in establishing or upgrading diagnostic laboratories
Proposed training areas

Practical, hands-on learning

Histopathology & Laboratory Techniques

Practical exposure to specimen handling, tissue processing, embedding, microtomy, staining, quality practices, and basic histopathology workflows.

Molecular Diagnostics

Fundamentals of molecular biology, PCR-based techniques, specimen management, contamination prevention, quality control, and interpretation of molecular results.

Laboratory Quality & Accreditation

Quality management systems, documentation, quality indicators, internal quality control, non-conformities, corrective actions, and accreditation preparedness.

Laboratory Operations & Management

Practical learning in workflow design, turnaround time, inventory management, equipment planning, team management, safety, and efficient laboratory operations.

Diagnostic Leadership & Professional Development

Programmes focused on communication, leadership, team building, decision-making, laboratory management, and developing the next generation of diagnostic professionals.

Our training philosophy

Knowledge, paired with experience

We believe that the best laboratory training combines knowledge with experience. Our programmes will emphasise practical demonstrations, real-world laboratory workflows, case-based learning, quality principles, and problem-solving rather than classroom theory alone.

The PathMole Training Institute will also aim to create opportunities for short-term observerships, workshops, focused certificate programmes, and continuing professional development as the initiative grows.

Our vision

A centre of excellence

To develop a centre of excellence for practical diagnostic education and laboratory professional development — helping create competent, confident, quality-conscious professionals who can contribute meaningfully to patient care.
