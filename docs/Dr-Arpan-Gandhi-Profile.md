## Professional Identity

Dr. Arpan Gandhi is a senior diagnostic medicine professional and healthcare strategist with nearly three decades of experience spanning clinical pathology, laboratory operations, healthcare strategy, M&A, quality systems, and board governance. He has worked across large corporate diagnostic chains, hospital networks, regional centers, and startup environments across India.

His career is defined by a belief that excellence in diagnostics is not about technology alone — it is about leadership, systems, quality, culture, and the ability to continuously adapt.

He views healthcare from multiple perspectives: **clinician, pathologist, laboratory leader, entrepreneur, healthcare strategist, and board member.**

### Phase 02 — Network Expansion: Expanding Reach
- Expanded diagnostics beyond metropolitan centres into urban and semi-urban regions
- Built satellite labs and collection centre networks

### Phase 03 — SCEH Hospital Network: Standardizing at Scale
- Led post-pandemic standardization across hospital networks

- Systems served hundreds of thousands of patients across multiple states

### Phase 04 — Diagnostic Services (Cofounder): Mergers & Acquisitions
- Co-founded a diagnostic services company
- Led acquisition and operational integration of **four regional diagnostic centers** in tier 2 and tier 3 cities
- Each center had its own culture, workflows, and legacy systems — successfully integrated into a seamless network
- Required equal parts clinical rigour and change management

### Phase 05 — Crisis Leadership & Mentorship
- Set up **COVID-19 RT-PCR laboratories** under unprecedented pressure during the pandemic
- Mentored **200+ residents and laboratory professionals** through structured training programs, clinicopathological case discussions, and direct guidance
